An independent, offline cover art studio for the photos that tell your story. Build a neon-sunset collage, reframe every panel, set your headline, and export a high-resolution PNG. Your photos stay on your device.

## Run it

Open [`app/index.html`](app/index.html) in a desktop browser, or serve the folder locally:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Choose a square, portrait, or 1920 × 1080 landscape poster. Add your photos, assign a different image to each panel, drag to reposition, and adjust the zoom and colour grade. The canvas exports a print-sharp PNG at the selected dimensions. No account, installation, upload, or network access is needed.

## About Afterglow

Afterglow is an independent cover art studio. It is an original project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games. Bundled sample photographs are provided by the make.fun contest starter.
